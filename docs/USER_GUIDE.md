# Pantry: how to use it

Pantry keeps track of the food and household supplies in your home. You scan things when you put them away, tap a button when you use something, and the app makes your shopping list for you.

**Open the app:** https://illusion1995.github.io/pantry/

---

## Getting started (one time)

1. On your phone, open the link above in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Sign in with Google** and choose your Google account.
3. Your pantry opens. If you see *"You're signed in as…"* instead, ask the person who set up the app to add your email in **Share this pantry**. Your pantry then opens by itself.
4. Put the app on your home screen: tap Chrome's **⋮** menu (top right), then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**. From now on, open Pantry from its yellow icon like any other app.

You stay signed in. You won't need to do this again.

---

## The home screen

- **Scan item** (the big yellow button): add things you bought.
- **My pantry** (with a number): everything you have. The number is how many different items are in stock.
- **Need to buy** (with a number): things you've run out of. The number turns red when something is on the list.

Below those are smaller links: **Add something without a barcode**, **Share this pantry** and **Back up my pantry**.

The phone's **Back** button, or the **‹ Back** button at the top of each screen, takes you back.

---

## Adding things you bought

1. Tap **Scan item**. The first time, the phone asks to use the camera. Tap **Allow**.
2. Hold the barcode inside the **yellow box**. The phone buzzes when it reads it.
3. Check the name. If it's wrong, tap **Change the name**.
4. Tap **−** or **+** to set **how many** you're adding. You can also tap the number and type it.
5. Tap **Add to pantry**.
6. Tap **Scan another item** to keep going, or **Done**.

**Tips**

- **In a dark pantry:** tap **Turn on the light** to use the phone's flashlight. The button only appears if the phone's camera has one.
- **Scanning something you already have:** it adds to the count. It won't make a second copy, and it keeps the name you gave it.
- **"The package name is in French":** the product only had a French name, so the app gave it a plain English one, like *Plain butter shortbreads* or *Hazelnut spreads*. The name from the package is shown above it. If you'd call it something else, tap **Change the name**.
- **"This barcode isn't in the product lists":** type what it is, once. Next time you scan it, the app remembers.
- **If the camera won't read a barcode:** tap **Type the barcode numbers instead** and type the numbers printed under the lines.

---

## Pictures

- **See a picture bigger:** tap any picture, in My pantry, Need to buy, or when adding or changing an item. Tap anywhere, or press the phone's **Back** button, to close it.
- **Add or change a picture:** tap the round yellow **pen** on the corner of the picture. If there's no picture yet, tap the **Add photo** box. Then choose:
  - **Take a photo** opens the camera. Take the picture and tap the check mark or **OK**.
  - **Choose from my photos** shows the pictures on your phone, including Google Photos.
- Your own photo is always shown instead of the one the app found.
- **Remove my photo** (in the same menu) goes back to the app's picture, if there was one.
- A new picture is saved when you tap **Add to pantry** or **Save changes**.

## Adding something without a barcode

For fruit and vegetables, bulk bins, home-canned food, or anything else without a barcode:

1. On the home screen, tap **Add something without a barcode**. You can also tap **No barcode? Type the name** on the scan screen.
2. Type what it is, for example *Potatoes*.
3. If it's already in the app, it shows up underneath. Tap it to add to it.
4. Tap **Next**. For something new, tap **Food** or **Household**. Choose how many, and tap **Add to pantry**.

---

## Food and Household

Your things are kept in two groups:

- **Food:** everything you eat and drink.
- **Household:** cleaning supplies (like CLR and dish soap), paper towels, toiletries and pet food.

When you scan something, the app usually knows which group it belongs to. If it doesn't, it asks **Where does it go?**. Tap **Food** or **Household**, then **Add to pantry**.

In **My pantry**, tap **Food** or **Household** at the top to switch between them. The app remembers which one you looked at last. If you search on one tab and the item is on the other, tap **Look in Food** or **Look in Household**.

**Something in the wrong group?** Tap its name, then tap **Food** or **Household** under **Where does it go?**, then **Save changes**. Everything you added before this was set up is under **Food**, so move any cleaning supplies over this way.

**Need to buy** is still one list, with a Food part and a Household part, so one shopping trip covers both.

---

## When you use something

1. Tap **My pantry**.
2. Find the item. Type in **Search your pantry** to find it quickly.
3. Tap **−** once for each one you used.

When you use the **last one**, the count turns into a red **0** and the item goes on your **Need to buy** list by itself.

**Tapped by mistake?** Tap **Undo** in the message at the bottom of the screen, or tap **+** to put it back.

---

## Your shopping list (Need to buy)

Tap **Need to buy** to see everything you've run out of. You don't have to add anything yourself.

- **Send the list to someone:** tap **Share list**, then choose how to send it (Messages, Gmail, and so on).
- **After shopping:** scan the items as you put them away, and they come off the list by themselves. For things without a barcode, tap **Bought it** next to the item and say how many.

---

## Fixing a mistake

In **My pantry** or **Need to buy**, tap an item's **name** to open **Change item**. There you can:

- **Fix the name**, then tap **Save changes**.
- **Fix how many you have**, then tap **Save changes**.
- **Change the picture** with the yellow pen, then tap **Save changes**.
- **Move it** between **Food** and **Household**, then tap **Save changes**.
- **Delete item**, for something you don't buy anymore. It's removed from the pantry and the shopping list.

---

## Sharing the pantry with family

Everyone you add sees the same pantry and the same shopping list. A change on one phone shows up on the others.

1. On the home screen, tap **Share this pantry**.
2. Under **Add someone**, type their Google (Gmail) email address.
3. Tap **Add person**.
4. They open the app on their phone and tap **Sign in with Google**, and the pantry opens for them.

To stop sharing with someone, tap **Remove** next to their email.

---

## No internet?

The app still works. You can scan, add and use things as usual. Changes are sent as soon as the phone is back online. The one exception: without internet the app can't look up the name of a new barcode, so it asks you to type it.

---

## Backups (optional)

Your pantry is saved online, so it's safe even if a phone is lost. For extra peace of mind, tap **Back up my pantry**, then **Save a backup**. The file goes to your phone's Downloads. **Choose a backup file** puts a saved backup back. That replaces everything in the pantry, so the app asks first.

---

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "The camera is blocked" | Open the phone's **Settings → Apps → Chrome** (or Pantry) **→ Permissions → Camera** and choose **Allow**. Then tap **Try the camera again**. |
| The camera won't read a barcode | Hold the phone a little farther away and keep it still. In a dark spot, turn on the light. Or tap **Type the barcode numbers instead**. |
| "That change couldn't be saved" | Check that the phone has internet and try again. If it keeps happening, tell the person who set up the app. |
| "You're signed in as…" and no pantry | Ask the person who set up the app to add your email in **Share this pantry**. |
| The app looks different after an update | That's normal. Close the app and open it again. |

Need help? Ask the person who set up the app for you.
